Our Mission

In fast-paced cities, finding hygienic, affordable, home-style food daily is a constant challenge. On the flip side, thousands of talented local cooks and mess operators struggle with manual record-keeping, lost payments, and inefficient delivery routines.

TIFFIN FLOW was built to digitize and automate the entire tiffin supply chain. We empower consumers with full control over their meals while helping local food businesses scale effortlessly through our automated SaaS platform.

For Meal Subscribers

  • No rigid contracts—flexibility to pause anytime
  • Transparent daily meal logging
  • Hygienic and verified provider network

For Tiffin Providers

  • Automated customer ledger and payment reminders
  • Optimized daily delivery route mapping
  • Zero paperwork for daily count management
